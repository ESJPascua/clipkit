Where's your audience?

Not sure? Check the audience or "top locations" tab in your analytics after a week. English streamer and podcast clips usually lean US.

Best windows

Good to know

  • Evenings win almost everywhere. Most people scroll after school or work.
  • Post 30–60 minutes before the window so the clip is already being tested when the rush starts.
  • Mixed audience? Pick the biggest country for your best clip and the second one for your next.
  • These are starting points. Studies disagree a lot, so after a week trust your own analytics.
